Key Hierarchy Concepts
RBAC Inheritance: Assignments flow DOWN. Owner at MG = Owner on everything below.
Azure Policy: Also inherits down. Policies at MG enforce on all child subscriptions.
Resource Group: Shared lifecycle. Delete the RG = delete all resources in it.
Subscription: Primary billing boundary. Moving resources between subscriptions requires specific steps.
Management Group: Up to 6 levels deep. Root MG is created automatically per tenant.
