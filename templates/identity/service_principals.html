Service Principals & Managed Identities

Non-human identities for applications and Azure services
SP vs Managed Identity
Service Principal (Application)
Created when you register an app in Entra ID. You manage credentials (client secret or certificate). Used for DevOps pipelines, external apps, and cross-tenant scenarios.
Managed Identity (preferred)
Azure automatically creates and manages the credentials. No secrets to store or rotate. Two types:
  • System-assigned: Tied to a specific resource. Deleted when resource is deleted.
  • User-assigned: Independent resource. Can be assigned to multiple VMs/services.
Use Managed Identity wherever possible — eliminates credential management risk.
For DevOps pipelines, Service Principals with federated credentials (OIDC) are preferred over client secrets.
App ID (Client ID) uniquely identifies the service principal in the tenant.
