Role Assignments

Who has access to what — the heart of Azure RBAC
A Role Assignment = Principal (who) + Role Definition (what permissions) + Scope (where). Assignments are additive and inherit DOWN the scope hierarchy. Azure defaults to DENY if no assignment grants access.
Create Role Assignment
Scope Inheritance
MG Management Group → broadest
Sub Subscription
RG Resource Group
Res Resource → narrowest
Assignments at broader scopes inherit down. Owner at MG = Owner on everything below it. Prefer narrower scopes (least-privilege).
