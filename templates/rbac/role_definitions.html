Role Definitions

Built-in and custom roles — what permissions each role grants
A Role Definition lists the set of permissions: Actions (control plane), DataActions (data plane), and exclusions via NotActions/NotDataActions. Effective permissions = (Actions − NotActions) + (DataActions − NotDataActions).

Owner vs Contributor: Key difference is role assignment ability. On the exam, if you need to grant resource management WITHOUT ability to assign roles, use Contributor.
Custom roles require Owner or User Access Administrator to create. Max 5,000 per tenant.
DataActions (storage blob, queue, etc.) vs Actions (control plane) — Storage Blob Data Contributor ≠ Storage Account Contributor.
